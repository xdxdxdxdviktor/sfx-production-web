Editing and post

Video editing and post-production

Already have footage? We turn it into a story that keeps viewers watching to the end.

  • Colour
  • Sound
  • Subtitles
  • 3–14 working days
Request this serviceFAQ
00:00:12:05

We edit corporate videos, YouTube and social content, weddings, events and longer documentary pieces. Send us footage from a camera or phone and we cut it into a video with rhythm, story and clean sound.

Post-production includes colour grading to match shots from different cameras, sound clean-up and mixing, subtitles and motion graphics. One edit gives you landscape and vertical versions.

Who it’s for

  • Companies with their own footage
  • Creators and influencers
  • Agencies needing extra hands
  • Weddings and family videos

What’s included

  • Footage hand-over and backup
  • Edit to script or from raw footage
  • Colour grading and shot matching
  • Sound clean-up and mix
  • Subtitles and captions
  • Two rounds of feedback
  • Exports for every channel

What you get

  • Finished video in 4K or Full HD
  • Social versions
  • Project archived for later changes
Typical delivery: 3–14 working days

Equipment and software

  • 02

  • 03

  • 04

FAQ

What format should we send?

Original files from the camera or phone are fine. We take them via cloud storage or in person.

How much does video editing cost?

It depends on scope, timing and complexity. After a short call we send you a tailored quote, free and non-binding.

How many revision rounds are included?

Two rounds of feedback. Further changes are agreed by scope.

Do you do vertical short videos?

Yes, one shoot can give you versions for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Can you do English subtitles?

Yes, in Czech and English.
