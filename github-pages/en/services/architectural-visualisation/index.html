Visualisation

From drawings and material specs we create photorealistic exterior and interior visualisations. Buyers see how the house looks at sunset, how light falls into the living room and which materials the architect chose.

We can composite the visualisation into a real photo or aerial shot of the site, prepare material and time-of-day variants and a smooth fly-through of the whole project. Use the results on the project website, in brochures and on billboards.

Who it’s for

  • Developers selling projects
  • Architects and interior designers
  • Municipalities and competitions
  • Private investors

What’s included

  • Drawing hand-over and view planning
  • 3D model of the building or interior
  • Materials, lighting and surroundings
  • Photorealistic renders
  • Post-production and detail work
  • Two rounds of feedback

What you get

  • High-resolution visualisations for web and print
  • Optional 4K fly-through
  • Material and time-of-day variants
Typical delivery: 7–20 working days

FAQ

What input do you need?

Drawings, sections and material lists. 3D data makes it faster.

How much does a visualisation cost?

An interior view starts around €180, an exterior around €240. Number of views, detail and animation drive the price. Use the calculator.

How many views do you recommend?

It depends on the goal. For sales, a few key exteriors and interiors usually do.

Can you place it in the real surroundings?

Yes, we can composite it into a photo or aerial shot of the actual site.

How fast will it be ready?

Usually 1–3 weeks depending on the number of views and the input data.
