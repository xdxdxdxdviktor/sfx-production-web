We produce music videos, short films, documentaries and commercials with a larger crew. We handle the budget, crew, locations, permits and running the shoot days.

Together with the director and DoP we shape the look from moodboard to final grade. We can deliver the whole production, or just the gear and people your project needs.

Who it’s for

  • Short films and student projects
  • Music videos
  • Documentaries and portraits
  • Commercials with a larger crew

What’s included

  • Production plan and budget
  • Crew assembly
  • Locations and permits
  • Running the shoot days
  • Full post-production
  • Cinematic colour grading

What you get

  • Finished film or music video in cinema quality
  • Versions for festivals, web and social
  • Trailer and stills for promotion
Typical delivery: 3–8 weeks depending on scope

How it works

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We talk through the goal, audience and budget, then propose a format and scope that makes sense.

    OutputA proposal and tailored quote
  2. 2

    Pre-production

    Script, storyboard, locations, flight permits and the shoot-day schedule.

    OutputScript, storyboard and schedule
  3. 3

    Shoot

    Filming, photography, drone flights or 3D scanning. One person on set runs it all.

    OutputFootage and photos in the can
  4. 4

    Post and delivery

    Editing, grading, sound and export in formats for web, social and the big screen.

    OutputFinished files in every format
