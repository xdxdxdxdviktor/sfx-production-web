360° / Matterport

Matterport 360° virtual tours

Clients walk through the space as if they were there. Any time, from anywhere, without a site visit.

  • Price from €140
  • 1–3 working days
Estimate the priceRequest this service
Drag to look around

A Matterport virtual tour is a 3D model of a space that visitors move through freely in the browser on desktop and mobile. They understand layout, size and flow better than from photos.

We scan the space in a single visit, add a floor plan, labels and points of interest, and hand over a link and embed code for your website or listing. Tours cut wasted viewings and bring buyers who have already decided.

Who it’s for

  • Property sales and rentals
  • Showrooms and shops
  • Hotels, restaurants and guesthouses
  • Facility management and documentation

What’s included

  • 3D scan of the space
  • Interactive tour with dollhouse view
  • Link and embed code for your website
  • Labels and points of interest
  • Stills of selected views
  • Hosting for an agreed period

What you get

  • Tour link for listings and website
  • HD stills
  • Optional floor plan with dimensions
Typical delivery: 1–3 working days

FAQ

How long does scanning take?

It depends on size. A flat takes a single visit.

How much does a Matterport tour cost?

A flat starts around €140. Larger spaces are priced by floor area. Get a guide price in the calculator.

Where does the tour run?

In the browser on desktop and mobile. Embed it or share a link.

Does the space need to be empty?

No, just tidy. The tour shows it as clients will see it.

How long will the tour stay online?

We agree hosting as needed, usually for the sale or rental period.
