We film commercials, corporate and recruitment videos, event coverage and regular content for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Before the camera rolls we agree who the video is for and what the viewer should do when it ends.

The whole production is under one roof: concept and script, a shoot day with camera, lighting and sound, editing, colour grading, subtitles and music. One shoot gives you versions for every channel you use.

Who it’s for

  • Brands introducing a product or team
  • Event and conference organisers
  • Marketing teams needing regular social content
  • Cultural and non-profit projects

What’s included

  • Kick-off call and concept proposal
  • Script, storyboard and shoot schedule
  • Shoot day with camera operator, lighting and sound
  • Editing, colour grading and sound mix
  • Licensed music or your own
  • Two rounds of feedback
  • Exports in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1

What you get

  • Finished video in 4K
  • Short cut-downs for social
  • Thumbnail image
  • Subtitles if you need them
Typical delivery: 7–20 working days depending on scope

FAQ

How long does a video take?

A short social video is usually ready within a week of the shoot. A corporate film or commercial takes 2–4 weeks including prep. We confirm the timeline in the quote.

How much does a corporate video cost?

Short social videos start around €360, a corporate video is typically €1,000–2,400 and a commercial from €1,600. Shoot days, crew size and post-production drive the price. Get a guide price in the calculator above.

Can we supply our own music and logo?

Yes. We use your assets, or source licensed music and animate your logo.

Do you shoot vertical video for Reels and TikTok?

Yes. We frame shots so the same footage works in landscape and vertical.

Who writes the script?

We prepare the script and storyboard around your goal. If you have your own, we’re happy to work with it.
