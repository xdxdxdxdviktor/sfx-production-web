From the first call to finished files. You know what happens at each step and what you get from it.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We talk through the goal, audience and budget, then propose a format and scope that makes sense.

    OutputA proposal and tailored quote

About me

SFX production is one person. You talk to me from the first message to the final delivery.

Camera · photo · post-production

Hi, I’m Jirka.

I film, photograph and edit. What I enjoy most is finding a different way to show things: with light, camera movement or a view from a drone. I’m personally on every job, from the first meeting to the final export.

For bigger projects I bring in people I know and trust: a second camera operator, a sound recordist or a 3D artist. But you still deal with just me.

A good shot doesn’t happen by accident. It comes from preparation, light and patience.
