Privacy policy

This policy explains how we handle personal data you send us through the enquiry form or by email.

Controller

to be added, IČO to be added, registered at to be added. Contact: sfx-production@seznam.cz.

What we process

Your name, email, phone (if provided), company and the project details you enter in the form.

Why and on what basis

Only to handle your enquiry and prepare a quote. The legal basis is steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

How long

As long as needed to handle the enquiry. If no cooperation follows, we delete the data within to be added (e.g. 1 year). If it does, we keep it for the period required by accounting and tax law.

Who has access

Only us and the providers we need to operate: website hosting, email and FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers the enquiry from the form to our inbox. We don’t sell data or share it for marketing.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your data. Just email sfx-production@seznam.cz. You can also complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
