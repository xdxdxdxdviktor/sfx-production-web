How to shoot a recruitment video people outside the industry enjoy

Factory, warehouse or workshop. How to show the work so the video attracts new people, and how to film in full operation without holding anyone up.

A recruitment video exists to attract new employees. So put the people who run the machines in front, not the machines.

Planning the day

You film during normal operation. Plan the schedule with shift leaders so nobody waits and the most interesting work is still captured. Safety comes first: the crew gets the same briefing and protective gear as any visitor.

Interviews

Two or three employees say in their own words why they work there. No script, just questions and their answers. Honesty is what convinces viewers.

Delivery

Shoot two versions straight away: a longer one for the careers site and a 30-second cut for social ads. A short vertical version performs best on social.
