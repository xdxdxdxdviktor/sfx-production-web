Matterport or regular photos?

When a virtual tour pays off and when a good photo set is enough. Practical differences when selling and renting flats.

A virtual tour doesn’t replace photos, it complements them. The question isn’t either-or, but when a tour is worth it.

When a tour makes sense

For higher-priced properties, buyers from out of town, and layouts that don’t read well in photos. Buyers walk through on their own and arrive at the viewing already decided.

When photos are enough

For smaller flats and quick sales. Light, a tidy space and the right angles matter most. A well-shot flat with a floor plan often sells itself.

What we recommend

Both: photos for the listing, the tour as a link for serious buyers, and an aerial shot if the property has land or interesting surroundings.
