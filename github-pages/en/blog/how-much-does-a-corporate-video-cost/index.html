How much does a corporate video cost?

From a short social clip to a TV spot. What drives the price of a video and how to set a budget without paying for things you don’t need.

The price of a video isn’t about length, it’s about the work behind it. A one-minute video can cost €200 or €4,000. The difference is preparation, shoot days, crew size and how much post-production it needs.

What drives the price most

  • Shoot days. Each extra day means a camera operator, gear and travel.
  • Crew size. A second camera, sound recordist or gaffer raise quality and cost.
  • Preparation. Script, storyboard, casting and locations.
  • Post-production. Editing, grading, motion graphics, subtitles and music.
  • Number of versions. Every extra vertical or language version is extra work.

Typical price levels

Short social videos usually cost €360–600. A corporate or recruitment video is most often €1,000–2,400. A commercial with prep and a larger crew starts around €1,600 and can go much higher.

How to save without looking cheap

Shoot more content in one day. One shoot can give you the main video and several short versions for social, which brings the cost per video down. A clear brief helps too: the better you know what the video should do, the less time goes on shots nobody needs.
